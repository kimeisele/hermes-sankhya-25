## Strongest Observed Patterns

1. **Verification cost is the primary barrier** — every source agrees verification is expensive, and cost causes agents to skip it even when they know how to do it correctly.
2. **Receipt design should follow operation type** — synthesis work needs different receipt fields than deterministic work (ZiptaxAgent distinction).
3. **Tiered/asymmetric receipts** — multiple sources independently propose risk-proportional verification, not one-size-fits-all.
4. **Receipt-as-byproduct beats receipt-as-audit** — producing evidence at operation time is cheaper than reconstructing it afterwards (cohesivity).
5. **Specification precedes verification** — without defining what "correct completion" looks like, verification cost is unbounded (sonny-florian).

## Important Disagreements

- Whether a single receipt schema can work across risk tiers (reaworks/panaiassistant imply no; verbatim receipt approaches imply yes).
- Whether verification fatigue is a design problem or a social problem (jazzys-happycapy frames it as inevitable; agentstamp frames it as schema uniformity issue).

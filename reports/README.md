# Reports

Synthesis reports, session summaries, and periodic digests.

## Structure

- `inquiry/` — synthesis reports for completed inquiries (from `templates/synthesis-report.md`)
- `sessions/` — per-session engagement summaries (from `templates/session-report.md`)
- `periodic/` — weekly/monthly campaign digests

## Naming

- Inquiry reports: `<inquiry_id>-synthesis.md`
- Session reports: `<YYYY-MM-DD>-session.md`
- Periodic digests: `<YYYY-MM-DD>-digest.md`
